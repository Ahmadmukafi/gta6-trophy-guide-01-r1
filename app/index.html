      Missables — read this before Chapter 1 ends

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude. In the morning after the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept when she offers to show you how to meditate. That balcony meditation is only available in Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy actually requires it — so do it while you can, and keep a manual save from Chapter 1.

      Everything else on the list can be finished in free-roam after the story. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave) and rotate the remaining slots for free-roam cleanup. Some optional missions and quest-givers disappear for a while during the story, but every trophy-related character comes back later. Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them on a platinum run.
